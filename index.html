About Us

Real Estate Guidance Built Around Trust

B.H Akhade Real Estate is a property brokerage and consultancy business focused on providing personalized and transparent real estate guidance across Mumbai.

With its local base in Ghatkopar and service reach extending from Andheri to Thane, the consultancy works with property buyers, sellers, investors, owners and stakeholders exploring redevelopment opportunities.

Our approach is simple — understand the client's requirement, identify suitable opportunities, communicate clearly and provide dedicated assistance throughout the property journey.

"At B.H Akhade Real Estate, every property requirement is approached with personal attention, clear communication and a commitment to helping clients navigate their real estate decisions with confidence."
